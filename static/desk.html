      Responsible use

      Public sources only. Signals come from published news via NewsAPI, and profiles from Wikipedia and Wikidata. Court, filing and deed sources are planned. No scraping against a site's terms, no purchased personal data.

      Sensitive events go through intermediaries. For a death, divorce or financial distress, the first contact is the estate attorney, counsel or advisor, never the family directly.

      Human judgment decides. The score ranks research effort. A specialist reviews every lead before any outreach.

      Privacy law applies. Clients in the EU and UK fall under GDPR; profiling needs a lawful basis and data should be minimized and deleted when stale.

      MIT Entertainment, Media & Sports Lab action learning project. Leads are drawn from live news coverage; confirm every signal against the original record before any outreach.

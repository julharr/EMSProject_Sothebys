      Methods & data

      How a news story becomes either a consignment lead or a tagged buyer profile, and where each piece of data would come from.

      1Pull

      News API every 30 minutes (48 requests a day), plus a one-request-per-day catch-up for the last week.

      2Split

      Estate, family and financial-change stories, and announced sales, become consignment signals. IPOs, company sales, payouts and new homes become buy signals.

      3Consignment

      Capture only the item, the person and a one-line summary. No further research runs.

      4Profile builder

      For buyers: four sources plus a Claude pass produce tags, each tied back to the person and their prior stories.

      Collecting tags and the net-worth bar

      A collecting tag is only applied when there is evidence of the interest and the person's net worth clears the bar for that category's tier. Evidence below the bar is kept, struck through, so a specialist can see it.

      Wealth and affiliation markers

      Markers carry no net-worth bar. Each one records which source produced it.

      Responsible use

      Dummy data. Every person, story, ranking and source finding on this page is fictional, made up to show the concept.

      Consignment stops early on purpose. Estate, family and financial-change stories are sensitive; the desk records only what the story says and routes first contact through an intermediary.

      Tags are research leads, not facts. Each tag shows its source so a specialist can verify it before any outreach.

      Privacy law applies. Profiling under GDPR and similar laws needs a lawful basis; keep data minimal and delete it when stale.
